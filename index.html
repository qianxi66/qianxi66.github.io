About me

Hi! I’m Bo Sun, an M.S. student in Computer Science at Northeastern University’s Silicon Valley campus. I’m also a research assistant at the Human-Centered AI Lab, advised by Prof. Dakuo Wang.

My research focuses on how people and AI systems work together, including using LLM agents to simulate real users[1][5], testing how well LLM agents collaborate with each other[6], and designing AI tools that clinicians and educators can trust[2][3][4]. I build the systems I study, from multi-agent simulation frameworks to clinical dashboards and voice agents for patients.

Before Northeastern, I got my B.Eng. in Information Security at Beijing University of Technology. I also did summer research at the University of Notre Dame through the iSURE program.

Projects

CollabSim

Human-Centered AI Lab, Northeastern

A simulation framework for testing how well LLM agents build common ground, keep a shared picture of the task, and repair misunderstandings. I wrote the experiment controller, the module that asks agents what they think after every action, and the model layer that runs OpenAI, Anthropic, and self-hosted models in parallel.

Remote monitoring for cancer treatment-induced cardiotoxicity

Human-Centered AI Lab, Northeastern

I built the full-stack clinician dashboard and the backend that brings smartwatch data and voice check-ins together, and I developed and published the Alexa skill that patients talk to for daily symptom check-ins.

AI sepsis risk dashboard for physicians

Northeastern

An Epic-style dashboard that shows a sepsis risk forecast, its uncertainty, and a what-if lab test simulation. I designed and built the dashboard, integrated the prediction model, and took part in a study with 12 physicians.

DPRF

Human-Centered AI Lab, Northeastern

A framework that refines an LLM agent’s persona step by step until its behavior matches a real person’s. I implemented the framework and built the data pipeline for a new interview dataset.

SEAM

iSURE program, University of Notre Dame

A dashboard that helps teaching assistants, instructors, and advisors review student records together and reach conclusions that fit their role. I implemented the system, improved its interface, and helped design and run user studies with 27 staff.
