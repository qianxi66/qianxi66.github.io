About me

Hi! I’m Bo Sun, an M.S. student in Computer Science at Northeastern University’s Silicon Valley campus. I’m also a research assistant at the Human-Centered AI Lab, advised by Prof. Dakuo Wang.

My research focuses on how people and AI systems work together, including using LLM agents to simulate real users[1][3], testing how well LLM agents collaborate with each other[2], and designing AI tools that educators and clinicians can trust[4][5][6]. I build the systems I study, from multi-agent simulation frameworks to clinical dashboards and voice agents for patients.
